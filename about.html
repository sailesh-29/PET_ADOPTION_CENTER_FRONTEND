About Paw & Ledger

Paw & Ledger Pet Adoption Center was founded to give shelter animals a clear, well-documented path from intake to a permanent home. We keep a detailed record — a ledger — for every animal in our care, and we use that same care and transparency in how we work with adopters.

Our Objectives

  1. Provide accurate, up-to-date information about every pet available for adoption.
  2. Offer a simple and secure registration and login system for adopters.
  3. Guide adopters through a clear, step-by-step adoption process.
  4. Share announcements about adoption drives, vaccination camps, and events.
  5. Deliver a responsive experience that works on both desktop and mobile devices.

How Adoption Works

  • Step 1 — Register: Create an adopter account with your details.
  • Step 2 — Browse: Explore pets currently available on the Pets page.
  • Step 3 — Apply: Contact us about the pet you're interested in.
  • Step 4 — Meet & Greet: Visit the center to meet your potential companion.
  • Step 5 — Take Home: Complete the paperwork and welcome your new pet.

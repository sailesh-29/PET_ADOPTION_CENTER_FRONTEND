Welcome to Paw & Ledger

Every animal at our center has a story, and we keep a careful record — a "ledger" — of each one, from intake to adoption day. Our mission is to match every pet with a family who will love them for life, while making the adoption process simple, transparent, and welcoming.

Browse pets currently available, learn about our adoption process, and register to start your own adoption journey today.

Why Adopt With Us?

  • Every pet is vaccinated, health-checked, and behaviour-assessed before listing.
  • Our adoption counsellors help match your lifestyle with the right companion.
  • Post-adoption support and check-ins for the first few months.
  • A transparent, ledger-style record of every pet's journey through the center.
